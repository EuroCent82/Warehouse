Developer note - Unofficial community mods

The mods and links in this thread are shared by their creator. We don’t review or test these files and can’t verify their safety or compatibility. Any Discord server linked here is run independently from the studio.

Back up your saves before installing any mods. Mods can corrupt your save files or prevent the game from launching, even if they worked before a game update. We can’t recover corrupted saves or fix issues caused by mods.

**Warehouse** changes how many storages a stock manager can keep stocked in **Pionero Capital**. It is a PineLoader mod. The player still picks the storages.

**1.0.2** needs **PineLoader 1.1.0-alpha**.

Stock Management **I** stays at the game’s 4. **II** is 6. **III** is 8. Extra storages are not assigned on their own. Storages already assigned stay. `Enabled=false` in `Config\Warehouse.ini` puts the limit back to the game’s number. A missing `Middle` or `High` falls back to 6 and 8.

The trunk, pay, driving, crafting, recruiting, marketing, and work speed stay as the game set them.

**Install**
Install PineLoader first and close the game. Copy the `Warehouse` folder into `PineLoader\Mods`. It needs `Warehouse.dll` and `warehouse-agent.jar`.
